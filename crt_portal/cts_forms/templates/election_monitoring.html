History of Election Monitoring

Since 1965, the Department of Justice has sent federal observers and Civil Rights Division personnel to monitor elections nationwide, ensuring compliance with the Voting Rights Act and other federal laws. This record combines the Division’s chronological attorney-coverage listing, from 2000 to the present, its listing of federal observer deployments from 1965 onward, and the Voting Section’s 2026 primary monitoring summary.
